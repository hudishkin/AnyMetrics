Credits are for generation inside AnyMetrics. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

AI generation

You describe what you want to track. The server uses that description, your device language, and your answers to follow-up questions to find a public source and build a metric. Saved metrics and their keys are not sent. A generated metric can be wrong. Read the request before you rely on the value.
